Why projection is faster
Pchild → parent[shared colors] = one witness

Build this table once from feasible child assignments. A parent coloring asks whether its shared colors appear in the table. It never loops over every child coloring.

Single-exponential DP

O(b · (k + 1)² · 3k+1) time, where b is the bag count and k is the obtained width. Dense tables use direct indexing; sparse tables use binary search.

The obtained width is an upper bound on the treewidth of the graph being decomposed (original or residual); the badge above says whether it is proven optimal. These bounds describe the DP recurrence. Local constraint preparation adds O(b · (k + 1)² · log(n + 1)); construction is separate.
